About Bill Mulholland

Bill Mulholland writes intelligent, character-driven stories built around difficult evidence, flawed people, and the assumptions we make when the truth appears obvious. His fiction includes the Cecilia Raen thriller False Current and the speculative series The Unassigned, beginning with The Marker.

Drawing on more than two decades of medical experience, he brings a practical eye for human behavior, pressure, uncertainty, and consequence to his fiction. He also writes Christian nonfiction exploring faith, purpose, grief, perseverance, and what it means to live faithfully.

The truth is rarely simple—but it is always worth pursuing.
