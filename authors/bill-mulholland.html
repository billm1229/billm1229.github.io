Bill Mulholland

Stories built around evidence, pressure, and the human cost of certainty.

Bill Mulholland writes character-driven suspense and speculative fiction built around difficult evidence, flawed people, and the assumptions we make when the truth appears obvious. His fiction includes the Cecilia Raen novel False Current and The Unassigned series, beginning with The Marker.

He also writes Christian nonfiction focused on faith, purpose, perseverance, and the lasting significance of ordinary faithfulness.

The truth is rarely simple—but it is always worth pursuing.
