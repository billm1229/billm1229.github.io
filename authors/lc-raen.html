About L. C. Raen

L. C. Raen writes fiction for readers who want emotional tension, immersive atmosphere, and consequences that matter. Brine & Briar Books is home to Raen's work, including Saltbound.

The stories lean into the pull between beauty and danger—oaths, loyalty, desire, betrayal, and the price of choices made when there is no safe answer.
