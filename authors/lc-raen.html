L. C. Raen

Stories where desire, danger, and consequence collide.

L. C. Raen writes immersive fiction for readers who want emotional tension, vivid atmosphere, and choices that leave permanent marks. The stories move through worlds of loyalty, longing, betrayal, and impossible bargains—often with the sea close enough to taste in the air.

Brine & Briar Books is home to Raen's romantic and atmospheric fiction, including Saltbound.

Some oaths are written in salt. Some are paid in blood.
